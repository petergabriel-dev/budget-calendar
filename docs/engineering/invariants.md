# Invariants

Must-not-break rules below are verified from current code only.

## Balance is ledger-derived

- Account balance = `initial_balance + SUM(signed_amount)` for confirmed transactions. Account reads use this formula: `src/mobile/composeApp/src/commonMain/sqldelight/com/petergabriel/budgetcalendar/core/database/accounts.sq:6-21`, `src/mobile/composeApp/src/commonMain/sqldelight/com/petergabriel/budgetcalendar/core/database/accounts.sq:60-75`.
- Spending-pool membership derives from `AccountType`: only `CHECKING` and `CASH` are included; `SAVINGS`, `CREDIT_CARD`, and `INVESTMENT` are excluded. `Account.isInSpendingPool` is computed from its type: `src/mobile/composeApp/src/commonMain/kotlin/com/petergabriel/budgetcalendar/features/accounts/domain/model/Account.kt:3-30`.
- Spending-pool total uses the same confirmed ledger formula, with SQL selecting `a.type IN ('checking', 'cash')`: `src/mobile/composeApp/src/commonMain/sqldelight/com/petergabriel/budgetcalendar/core/database/budget.sq:1-13`.
- Do not reintroduce mutable account-balance adjustment. Transaction writes store ledger rows and emit invalidation; they do not mutate an account balance column: `src/mobile/composeApp/src/commonMain/kotlin/com/petergabriel/budgetcalendar/features/transactions/data/repository/TransactionRepositoryImpl.kt:103-181`.

## Safe-to-Spend formula

- STS = `pool balance - pending reservations - overdue reservations - credit-card reserved`, clamped `>= 0`: `src/mobile/composeApp/src/commonMain/kotlin/com/petergabriel/budgetcalendar/features/budget/domain/usecase/CalculateSafeToSpendUseCase.kt:18-20`.
- Confirmed spending is not subtracted separately; confirmed expenses are already reflected in computed pool balance, and `BudgetSummary` has no separate confirmed-spending field: `src/mobile/composeApp/src/commonMain/kotlin/com/petergabriel/budgetcalendar/features/budget/domain/model/BudgetSummary.kt:3-10`, `src/mobile/composeApp/src/commonMain/sqldelight/com/petergabriel/budgetcalendar/core/database/budget.sq:1-13`.
- Pending/overdue reservations include expenses from `CHECKING`/`CASH` accounts only; the predicate is `a.type IN ('checking', 'cash')`: `src/mobile/composeApp/src/commonMain/sqldelight/com/petergabriel/budgetcalendar/core/database/budget.sq:15-22`.
- Credit-card reserved = sum of each card's projected unpaid balance, clamped per card before summing: `max(0, -(initial_balance + SUM(signed_amount) WHERE status != 'cancelled'))`. This includes pending/overdue/confirmed charges and payments; `statement_balance`/`due_date` and statement-cycle days do not feed STS: `src/mobile/composeApp/src/commonMain/sqldelight/com/petergabriel/budgetcalendar/core/database/budget.sq:24-39`, `src/mobile/composeApp/src/androidUnitTest/kotlin/com/petergabriel/budgetcalendar/features/budget/domain/usecase/BudgetFlowObservationIntegrationTest.kt:804-950`.
- Payment-sheet submissions create confirmed paired transfers. Confirmed pool-cash decrease and card-reserve release therefore leave final STS unchanged: `src/mobile/composeApp/src/commonMain/kotlin/com/petergabriel/budgetcalendar/features/creditcard/domain/usecase/MakeCreditCardPaymentUseCase.kt:36-48`, `src/mobile/composeApp/src/androidUnitTest/kotlin/com/petergabriel/budgetcalendar/features/budget/domain/usecase/BudgetFlowObservationIntegrationTest.kt:804-950`.

## Credit-card displayed balance is projected

- Card displayed balance = `initial_balance + SUM(signed_amount WHERE status != 'cancelled')`; negative means owed, positive means overpaid credit. Query source: `src/mobile/composeApp/src/commonMain/sqldelight/com/petergabriel/budgetcalendar/core/database/credit_card_settings.sq:27-46`.
- Credit-card summaries are transaction-reactive and must re-query through `transactionChangedTrigger.onStart { emit(Unit) }.flatMapLatest { ... }`, not `combine(trigger)`: `src/mobile/composeApp/src/commonMain/kotlin/com/petergabriel/budgetcalendar/features/creditcard/domain/usecase/GetCreditCardSummariesUseCase.kt:24-61`.
- Summary `currentBalance` is projected; `reservedAmount = max(0, -projected)`; `availableCredit = creditLimit - abs(projected)`: `src/mobile/composeApp/src/commonMain/kotlin/com/petergabriel/budgetcalendar/features/creditcard/domain/usecase/GetCreditCardSummariesUseCase.kt:36-56`, `src/mobile/composeApp/src/commonMain/kotlin/com/petergabriel/budgetcalendar/features/creditcard/domain/usecase/GetCreditCardSummaryByIdUseCase.kt:26-52`.
- Accounts credit-card tile leads with available credit when a limit exists, then shows owed + live statement due + utilization; it must not replace section-total/net-worth math: `src/mobile/composeApp/src/commonMain/kotlin/com/petergabriel/budgetcalendar/features/accounts/presentation/components/CreditCardTile.kt:38-52`, `src/mobile/composeApp/src/commonMain/kotlin/com/petergabriel/budgetcalendar/features/accounts/presentation/components/CreditCardTile.kt:95-130`.
- Statement due is live display-only: `max(0, -(balance as of last close) - payments since close)` with due date from `paymentDueAfter`; null when cycle days are missing. It is distinct from total owed/current balance and does not feed STS or net worth: `src/mobile/composeApp/src/commonMain/kotlin/com/petergabriel/budgetcalendar/features/creditcard/domain/usecase/GetCreditCardSummariesUseCase.kt:64-72`.
- Net worth = non-card confirmed balances + signed projected card balances. Pending card charges lower net worth; pending spending-pool expenses do not until confirmed: `src/mobile/composeApp/src/commonMain/kotlin/com/petergabriel/budgetcalendar/features/accounts/data/repository/AccountRepositoryImpl.kt:124-128`.
